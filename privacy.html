Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy describes how the ViewWeave mobile app (“the App”) handles information. ViewWeave is a local multi-camera filming tool. It does not operate a ViewWeave cloud backend for your videos.

1. Who we are

ViewWeave is published as an independent app (bundle ID com.viewweave.app). Contact: support@viewweave.app.

2. Data the App stores on your device

  • Videos and library metadata created by you (sessions, takes, edits) — stored in the app’s private documents / media folders on the device.
  • License state (trial / Free / Basic) in a local file on the device.
  • Preferences such as export settings, also stored locally.

Uninstalling the App deletes this sandbox data. We do not receive a copy of your videos on ViewWeave servers.

3. Local network

When you run a multi-phone session, devices communicate on your local Wi‑Fi or hotspot (HTTP on the LAN) to sync capture and transfer rushes to the Master. This traffic stays on your network; it is not uploaded to a ViewWeave cloud.

4. Purchases (Basic)

Unlocking Basic uses Apple App Store / Google Play in-app purchase. Payment data is processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. ViewWeave does not receive your full payment card details.

5. What we do not do

  • No ViewWeave user account required to film.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No advertising ID tracking for ads in the App.
  • No analytics / crash cloud service is required for core filming (if platform diagnostics are enabled by you in iOS/Android settings, that is controlled by the OS vendors).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Do not use it to provide personal data of children to us.

7. Your choices

  • Delete library items inside the App.
  • Uninstall the App to remove local sandbox data.
  • Manage App Store / Play purchases and subscriptions (if any) in your store account settings.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.

8. Changes

We may update this page when the App’s practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised.

9. Politique de confidentialité (FR — résumé)

ViewWeave fonctionne en réseau local, sans cloud ViewWeave. Vos vidéos et votre bibliothèque restent sur vos appareils. L’achat Basic passe par Apple / Google. Contact : support@viewweave.app (remplacez par votre vraie adresse avant publication). Nous ne vendons pas vos données personnelles.
