Support

ViewWeave lets you film the same scene with several phones on a local Wi‑Fi or hotspot. One Master starts/stops recording; other phones join as Cameras. Footage stays on your devices — there is no ViewWeave cloud.

Common topics

  • Join session: same Wi‑Fi/hotspot as the Master, then scan the QR (or enter IP / link).
  • Library / rushes: stored in the app sandbox on the Master. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Basic license: 7-day Basic trial, then Free. Unlock Basic via in-app purchase (App Store / Play) or Restore purchases.
  • Free limits: up to 2 phones per session; Free exports use 720p with a small watermark.
